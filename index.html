04 — Student Support

Build your project.
Build your career.

We help students turn academic requirements into practical, real-world projects — from development and documentation through to internship support and the career documents that follow.

  1. 01

    Student Project Development

    BCA, MCA and other computer-stream projects, built with the technologies your syllabus and your career both care about — Flutter, Dart, Firebase, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Java and SQL.

  2. 02

    Project Documentation

    Project reports, presentations, database design and diagrams — the paperwork the submission is actually marked on.

  3. 03

    Internship Support

    Guidance and hands-on development work for students who want practical experience, not just a certificate.

  4. 04

    Genuine Internship & Career Documents

    Students who complete an actual internship or employment engagement with us may receive the appropriate company-issued documents for it — internship letter, offer letter, completion certificate or experience letter.

    Issued on the strength of the engagement, and only that. We do not sell letters, and we do not issue a document for work that did not happen.

  5. 05

    Portfolio & Resume Support

    A student portfolio worth sending: GitHub presented properly, an ATS-ready resume, and project write-ups a recruiter can read.

  6. 06

    Project Guidance & Deployment

    GitHub, Firebase, Supabase, hosting, deployment and testing — and a walkthrough of your own project, so the viva holds no surprises.
