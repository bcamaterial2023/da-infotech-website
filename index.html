Career & Documents

Career &
Resume Solutions.

Hiring is filtered by software before a person ever reads a page. These services are built around that reality.

  1. Resume Creation & Formatting

    A resume written and structured for the role being applied to, not a template with the name swapped out.

    • Resume creation from scratch
    • Professional resume formatting
    • Resume editing and rewriting
    • Resume conversion between formats

  2. ATS Optimization

    Applicant tracking systems parse before they rank. The document is built so it survives that pass intact.

    • ATS-friendly structure and layout
    • Keyword optimisation
    • Job-description-based tailoring
    • Parsing checks before delivery

  3. CV & Cover Letters

    The longer-form documents, and the letter that has to earn the first thirty seconds of attention.

    • CV creation and editing
    • CV formatting and cleanup
    • Cover letter support
    • Application-specific versions

  4. LinkedIn & Job Search

    The profile recruiters actually search, and support through the applying itself.

    • LinkedIn profile optimisation
    • Headline and summary rewriting
    • Job application support
    • Professional portfolio support

  5. PDF & Document Services

    Document work handled properly, including the PDF jobs that usually end in a broken layout.

    • PDF editing and formatting
    • PDF ↔ Word conversion
    • Merging, splitting, compression
    • Document formatting and cleanup

  6. Training & Project Support

    Hands-on development experience, project guidance, documentation and deployment — plus the company-issued documents that follow a real engagement.

    Documents are issued on the strength of the engagement, and only that. We do not sell letters, and we do not issue a document for work that did not happen.

These are services, not site features. Document, resume and PDF work is carried out by hand and returned to you — this website does not convert, edit or process files automatically.
